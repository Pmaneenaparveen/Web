INDIAN FESTIVALS

  • DIWALI

India is a country with many colorful festivals.People celebrate festivals with joy and happiness.Each festivals has its own traditions and customs.Festivals bring people together and spread unity.

1.Diwali

Diwali is the bright Festival of Lights.People wear new clothes, share sweet treats, and exchange gifts with loved ones.

2.onam

Onam is the grand 10-day state and harvest festival of Kerala, India, celebrated to welcome the annual homecoming of the legendary King Mahabali.

2.Holi

Holi is a vibrant Hindu spring festival that celebrates love, new life, and the triumph of good over evil.
